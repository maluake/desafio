# Settlement terms - when is the money from my sales deposited?

The date a sale is deposited (liquidação / recebimento) depends on the payment method and on the settlement plan the merchant contracted. Values are always credited net (valor líquido), i.e. after fees (MDR), rent, chargebacks and other contractual deductions. Deposits go to the registered settlement account (domicílio bancário).

## Typical terms

- Pix: same day, usually within seconds of approval (may vary up to one calendar day depending on the Pix model).
- Debit card: typically the next business day (D+1 útil).
- Credit card à vista (single payment): typically 30 days (D+30) in the standard plan, or 2 business days (D+2) in plans with automatic advance ("receba em 2 dias").
- Credit card in installments (parcelado lojista): each installment is paid every 30 days, unless the merchant contracts advance (antecipação) or "Receba Já", which pays the whole installment sale in a single deposit in 2 or 30 business days.

Deposits that fall on weekends or bank holidays are made on the next business day.

## How to check the exact date of a specific sale

The Getnet app (Minha Conta) and the merchant portal show the receivables calendar (agenda de recebíveis) with the expected date and net amount of every sale. Authenticated customers can also ask this assistant, which looks up their sales and settlement plan.
