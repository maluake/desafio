# Pix on the Getnet machine

## How it works

The merchant selects Pix on the Getnet machine, types the amount and the machine generates a dynamic QR Code. The customer scans it with their bank app and pays. The machine confirms the payment and issues the receipt. Getnet advertises that the money is credited to the merchant's account within seconds (around 10 seconds) after payment approval.

## Do I need a bank account to receive sales via Pix?

Yes. Pix sales made on the Getnet machine are settled into the merchant's account registered with Getnet as the receiving account (domicílio bancário / conta de recebimento). There is no way to receive Pix sales without a registered account in the merchant's name (CPF or CNPJ).
- The natural and integrated option is a Santander account: linking the machine to a Santander account lets the merchant follow receivables in an organised way and use management tools.
- The Pix key (chave Pix) used to receive must be registered with Getnet.
- Settlement timing: the money usually lands the same day; Santander notes that the time of receipt may vary by up to one calendar day depending on the Pix model the merchant is eligible for and the time the sale was approved.
- Whether a non-Santander account can be used for Pix settlement depends on the contracted plan - confirm with Getnet support or in the Getnet app.

## Fees

Pix on the machine may have its own fee per transaction depending on the plan; check the current conditions in the Getnet app or website.
