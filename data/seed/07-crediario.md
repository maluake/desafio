# Crediário Getnet

## How many installments can I split a sale into with the crediário?

With Crediário Getnet a sale can be split into up to 48 installments (até 48x) on the card machine. Important rules:
- The maximum number of installments actually available and the interest rates for the shopper are defined by the cardholder's issuing bank (banco emissor).
- The shopper must have available credit limit for the total amount of the purchase.
- For the merchant, the sale is received as a single amount (the issuer finances the shopper), which helps cash flow.
- Fee for the merchant: Getnet material from 2024 mentions a single fee of 3,45% per sale regardless of the number of installments (subject to change).
- Older communications (2020) mentioned up to 36x; the current limit is 48x according to the 2024 Getnet brochure.

## Difference from regular installments

Regular installments on the machine (parcelado lojista) go up to 12x, with the merchant receiving each installment monthly (unless advanced). Crediário (parcelado emissor) goes up to 48x, with interest paid by the shopper to the issuing bank.

## How to sell with crediário on the machine

1. On the machine choose Credit, then the Crediário option.
2. Type the amount and choose the number of installments (subject to issuer approval).
3. The shopper inserts/taps the card and enters the PIN.
4. If approved, the receipt shows the installment plan.
