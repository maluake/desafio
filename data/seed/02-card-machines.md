# Getnet card machines (maquininhas)

Getnet offers three main card machine models in Brazil. All accept debit, credit à vista and in installments (parcelado em até 12x on the machine, and longer plans with Crediário Getnet), contactless/NFC (aproximação), and Pix via QR Code.

## Get Mini

- Compact entry-level machine for small businesses and entrepreneurs.
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi and 3G chip.
- Colour display; issues digital receipts (comprovante digital by SMS/e-mail) - it does not print.
- Indicative rent: about R$ 14,90/month, waived (aluguel grátis) for monthly revenue from about R$ 2.000 (conditions vary).
- Warranty: 1 year from the adhesion date, according to the Get Mini manual.

## Get Clássica

- Traditional mid-range machine with a built-in printer.
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi and 3G chip.
- Touchscreen; prints paper receipts and also sends digital receipts.
- Accepts around 28 card brands; installments up to 12x.
- Indicative rent: about R$ 79,90/month, with zero rent (aluguel zero) for monthly revenue above about R$ 10.000. Offered to companies with CNPJ.

## Get Smart

- The most advanced model: a smart POS (Android) with a large touchscreen.
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi and 4G chip.
- Prints and sends digital receipts; gives access to business management apps (sales control, catalogue, etc.) installed on the machine.
- Indicative rent: about R$ 129,90/month, with zero rent for monthly revenue above about R$ 60.000. Available for companies with CNPJ.

## Get Clássica vs Get Smart - what is the difference?

| | Get Clássica | Get Smart |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Traditional POS with printer | Smart POS (Android) with printer and apps |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi + 3G | Wi-Fi + 4G |
| Screen | Touchscreen | Larger touchscreen, app-based interface |
| Management apps on the device | No | Yes |
| Indicative rent | ~R$ 79,90/month (zero above ~R$ 10k revenue) | ~R$ 129,90/month (zero above ~R$ 60k revenue) |
| Fees (MDR) | Practically the same for both models (e.g. debit around 0,85%-0,95% in public comparisons) | Same |
| Best for | Merchants who want a reliable printer machine at lower cost | Higher-volume merchants who want 4G, apps and management features |

In short: both accept the same payment methods and have very similar fees; the Get Smart adds 4G, Android apps and a richer interface, while the Get Clássica is cheaper to rent and has an easier rent waiver threshold. Values are indicative and change frequently - confirm on www.getnet.net.
