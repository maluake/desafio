# Online payments and e-commerce

Getnet offers online payment solutions for digital businesses: online checkout, payment APIs for developers (credit, debit, Pix, boleto and digital wallets), tokenisation, recurring payments/subscriptions and anti-fraud tools. Marketplaces can use split payments. Globally, Getnet provides omnichannel acquiring for Santander's merchants across Europe and the Americas.

# Digital account

Merchants can follow their receivables integrated with a Santander account, which centralises sales, deposits and payments. Linking the card machine to the Santander account gives an organised view of receivables and access to management tools.
