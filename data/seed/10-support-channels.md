# Getnet service channels (customer support contacts)

How to contact Getnet customer support (atendimento / Central de Atendimento): phone/WhatsApp number, app and portal.

- Getnet app (Getnet Brasil / Minha Conta): sales, receivables calendar, advance simulation, statements, payment links, machine maintenance requests.
- Merchant portal on the Getnet website: reports and account management.
- Service Center WhatsApp number (Central de Atendimento Getnet no WhatsApp): (11) 97694-6815 (per the Get Lite manual - confirm on the official site).
- Santander managers and branches, for customers with a Santander account.

## Machine maintenance and replacement

Maintenance or replacement of a faulty machine can be requested through the Getnet app or the Service Center WhatsApp. Machines are covered by warranty under the rental contract (Get Mini: 1 year from adhesion according to its manual).

## Security tips

Getnet never asks for your password, token or full card numbers by phone, e-mail or WhatsApp. Do not share these data with anyone, including this assistant.
