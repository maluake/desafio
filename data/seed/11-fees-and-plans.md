# Fees and plans

Getnet pricing combines:
- MDR fee (taxa de desconto) per transaction, which varies by payment method (debit, credit à vista, credit in installments, Pix), card brand, revenue tier and settlement term;
- machine rent (aluguel), which can be waived above a monthly revenue threshold that depends on the model;
- optional advance fees (antecipação) when receiving credit sales earlier.

Indicative public references (they change often): debit around 0,85%-1,89% depending on the plan and term, credit à vista higher than debit, and installments higher than à vista. Plans with receipt in 2 days (D+2) charge more than plans with receipt in 30 days (D+30), because the advance cost is embedded.

Always simulate on the official Getnet website or app and check the contract summary (sumário executivo) for the conditions that apply to you.
