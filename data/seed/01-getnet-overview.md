# Getnet overview

Getnet is a payments company of the Santander group. In Brazil it offers merchants (lojistas, from individual entrepreneurs/MEI to large companies) card machines (maquininhas), online payments, Pix, payment links, receivables management and receivables advance (antecipação de recebíveis), and a digital account integrated with Santander. Internationally, Getnet operates as Santander's global merchant acquiring and payments platform (site www.getnet.net/en).

## Product portfolio (Brazil)

- Card machines (maquininhas / POS): Get Mini, Get Clássica and Get Smart. They accept debit, credit (à vista and parcelado / installments), contactless (NFC/aproximação), digital wallets and Pix via QR Code.
- Pix na maquininha: generate a Pix QR Code on the Getnet machine and receive in seconds.
- Payment link (Link de Pagamento / Get Pay): sell remotely through WhatsApp, social networks or e-mail.
- WhatsApp payments: charge and accept installment payments directly inside WhatsApp conversations.
- Crediário Getnet: long installment plans (up to 48x) financed by the cardholder's issuing bank.
- Receivables advance (antecipação de recebíveis): receive credit-card sales earlier, automatically or on demand.
- E-commerce / online checkout and APIs for digital businesses.
- Management apps and portal: Getnet app ("Getnet Brasil" / Minha Conta) and the Getnet merchant portal to track sales, receivables, statements and request support.

## Important note about prices and fees

Fees (taxas/MDR), machine rent (aluguel) and promotional conditions change frequently and depend on monthly revenue (faturamento), settlement term and card brand. Always confirm the current conditions on the official website or in the Getnet app before contracting.
