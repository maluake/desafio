# Receivables advance (antecipação de recebíveis)

## What it is

Receivables advance lets the merchant receive credit card sales (à vista or in installments) before their original due date, instead of waiting 30, 60 or more days. In exchange, a discount fee (taxa de antecipação, proportional to the number of days advanced) is charged on the advanced amount. It is used to improve cash flow (capital de giro).

## How it works with Getnet

- Automatic advance (Antecipação Automática): contracted at adhesion or later through the Getnet Service Center. All eligible credit sales are advanced automatically (for example, credit sales paid in 2 business days). It can be cancelled by the customer at any time, except for amounts already processed for advance payment.
- On-demand advance (Antecipação Eventual / pontual): the merchant chooses which future receivables to advance, whenever needed, through Getnet channels (app, portal or capture devices). A simulator shows the net amount before confirming.
- Receba Já: installment sales are paid in a single deposit in 2 or 30 business days.
- The amount received is net of fees, rent, fines, chargebacks and other values provided in the contract.

## Costs

The advance fee varies by term, revenue and negotiation; it is shown in the simulator in the Getnet app / Minha Conta before confirming. Some taxes (IOF) may apply depending on the modality. Always simulate before advancing.

## Step by step (on demand)

1. Open the Getnet app or portal and go to Antecipação / Receivables.
2. Choose the receivables or the amount you want to advance.
3. Review the simulation (fee and net amount).
4. Confirm; the money is credited to your registered account.
