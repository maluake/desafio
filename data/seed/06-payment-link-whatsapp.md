# Payment Link (Link de Pagamento)

## What it is

The Getnet Payment Link lets you sell remotely without a card machine or website: you create a link with the amount and description and send it to the customer, who pays online.

## Can I sell through WhatsApp using the Payment Link?

Yes. The link can be shared on WhatsApp, social networks (Instagram, Facebook), SMS or e-mail. The customer opens the link and pays with:
- credit card à vista or in installments (parcelado),
- debit,
- Pix,
- digital wallets.

It is available both for companies (CNPJ) and individuals (CPF). One of the entry plans had zero monthly fee and R$ 1,00 per approved transaction plus the MDR fee (conditions may change - confirm on the official site).

## How to create a link

1. Access the Getnet app or portal and choose Link de Pagamento.
2. Enter the amount, description and payment options (e.g. installments allowed).
3. Generate the link and share it with the customer on WhatsApp.
4. Track payment status in the app; the sale follows the normal settlement terms.

## WhatsApp payments (paying inside the chat)

Getnet also enables payments directly inside WhatsApp conversations (in partnership with Meta's WhatsApp payments). Since October 2023 customers can pay in installments in WhatsApp, up to 12x (parcelado lojista, within the cardholder's credit limit).
